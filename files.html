🗂️ My Files

Upload study materials for each file type — every category below has its own Upload button. Uploaded files also appear in the Library page (PDFs open in the reader; images, videos, audio and documents have their own buttons there), and everything syncs to your account. Click a category header to open or close it.

Files are stored in this browser and synced to your cloud storage (Supabase) — open the site on any device and your files are there. Very large files (over 40 MB) stay on this device only.
